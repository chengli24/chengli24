Welcome to my homepage. I am currently an MPhil student at the Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (HKUST), supervised by <a href="https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=NUV28h8AAAAJ&hl=en"><code>Associate Prof. Dasa Gu</code></a> and co-supervised by <a href="https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=sB519ukAAAAJ&hl=en"><code>Chair Prof. Alexis Kai Hon Lau</code></a> (Head of ENVR, HKUST).  

I will be a PhD student at HKUST, majoring in IIP (AI, Data Science, and Remote Sensing) in Spring February 2027, supervised by <a href="https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=NUV28h8AAAAJ&hl=en"><code>Associate Prof. Dasa Gu</code></a> and <a href="https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=dcDrhzMAAAAJ&hl=zh-CN"><code>Chair Prof. Pan Hui</code></a> (IEEE Fellow, MAE, FREng, ACM Distinguished Scientist)（IEEE会士，欧洲科学院院士，英国皇家工程院外籍院士，ACM杰出科学家），co-supervised by <a href="https://ocac.zju.edu.cn/2020/0509/c50525a2094491/pagem.htm"><code>Prof. Delu Pan</code></a>（Academician of the Chinese Academy of Engineering）（中国工程院院士）and <a href="http://www.sdioi.com/ryxx/1541.html"><code>Prof. Peng Chen</code></a>（中国国家优青）.

My research interests focus on AI for Environment, Remote Sensing, Computer Vision, Autonomous Driving and Machine Learning. I am also a research assistant at AIR, Tsinghua University, and was an intern LLM autonomous driving algorithm engineer at Li Auto Inc. and Shanghai Innovation Institute, advised by <a href="https://air.tsinghua.edu.cn/en/info/1046/1652.htm"><code>Assistant Prof. Zhao Hao</code></a>, <a href="https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=E1aaa_kAAAAJ&hl=zh-CN"><code>Dr. Zhihui Hao</code></a>, and <code>Mr. Gaoqiang Wu</code>. I was selected for the Asian Future Leader Scholarship Program (AFLSP) for 2024–2026 (100 students per year from top Asian universities).
